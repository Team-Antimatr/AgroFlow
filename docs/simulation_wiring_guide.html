💡 How the Sensors & 2-Channel Relay Module Work in Simulation Simulation Electronics
🧪 Analog Sensors → Potentiometers (Pins A0 & A1)

Real TDS and Soil Moisture probes output 0V to 5V analog voltage. In Wokwi, potentiometers give the exact same 0–5V into the Arduino ADC:

  • Potentiometer 1 (TDS → Pin A0):
    • Left knob (≤ 500 ppm): Clean water → SG90 stays at 0° (Bypass).
    • Right knob (> 500 ppm): Saline alert → SG90 rotates to 90° (Filter)!
  • Potentiometer 2 (Soil Moisture → Pin A1):
    • Dry (< 30%): Permitted cycle.
    • Wet (≥ 60%): Safety Lock shuts off pump relays to protect roots.
⚡ 2-Channel 5V Relay Module (Pins 7 & 8)

Arduino pins cannot deliver enough current to run motor pumps directly. The 2-Channel Relay Module acts as the electronic switch:

  • Relay Channel 1 (Pin 7): Controls the Main Submersible Pump in Reservoir 1. In Wokwi, the relay clicks and lights up when water flows.
  • Relay Channel 2 (Pin 8): Controls the Liquid Fertilizer Dosing Pump in Reservoir 2. Pulses for 2-3 seconds to inject the 30 mL dose.
